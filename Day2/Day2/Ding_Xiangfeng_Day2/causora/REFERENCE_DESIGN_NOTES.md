# Causora — Original Design Translation Notes

The supplied videos were treated as a study of interaction quality, not as artwork, layouts, copy, or brand systems to reproduce.

## Principles carried forward

| Observed quality | Causora translation |
| --- | --- |
| Smooth context-preserving transitions | Each workflow stage moves along a persistent decision rail and a visible evidence thread. |
| Progressive disclosure | A conclusion appears first; source quote, formula, role scope, and variable meaning open only when requested. |
| Small, affirmative micro-feedback | Controls acknowledge the selected option, local state, Golden Run, and human decision with compact feedback. |
| Stable navigation | The desktop rail and mobile step navigator preserve orientation through the full decision sequence. |
| High-quality surface hierarchy | Fine borders, luminous data chambers, controlled depth, and restrained accent use produce premium contrast without noise. |

## Deliberately rejected

- No copied layouts, visual assets, typography, text, or brand treatments.
- No decorative 3D scenes, giant hero-only composition, liquid background, fast montage, or aggressive parallax.
- No high-saturation marketing palette competing with data semantics.
- No interaction that depends only on hover, colour, or animation.
- No visual treatment that makes a local mock result appear to be a live business decision.

## Causora-specific innovation

The **Evidence Thread** turns the decision sequence into a visible provenance system. It is not a generic progress bar: each stage reflects a different trust condition—inputs, constraints, code-bound comparison, role conflict, and human authority. This supports the product thesis that a business recommendation should remain inspectable from the final choice back to its sources.

The refined **Proof Engine** makes that thesis spatial: a source excerpt, a contract variable and a matrix result occupy three persistent, non-overlapping perspective stations. A luminous route travels through the gutters, not across the card text. Their emphasis changes with the active workflow stage. Unlike a decorative 3D scene, the object displays the same mock IDs and values as the decision UI and provides direct stage navigation. A human decision resolves the path into a visible seal. It uses original code-rendered geometry and avoids borrowed assets.
