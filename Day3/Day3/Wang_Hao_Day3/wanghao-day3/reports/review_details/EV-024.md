## Six-evidence audit for the linked renewal interpretation

The six requested evidence records are internally consistent in the frozen mock and agree with the preserved page-4 text:

1. **EV-014 — notice period:** exact quote supports `60 days` (`supplier_a_agreement.pdf`, p. 4; `causora_day1_mock.json` evidence EV-014).
2. **EV-019 — renewal term:** exact quote supports `24 months` (`supplier_a_agreement.pdf`, p. 4; `causora_day1_mock.json` evidence EV-019).
3. **EV-021 — renewal price increase:** exact quote supports `14%` (`supplier_a_agreement.pdf`, p. 4; `causora_day1_mock.json` evidence EV-021). The API wire representation is `0.14` (`contract.renewalPriceIncreasePct`).
4. **EV-024 — minimum purchase share:** exact quote supports `60%` (`supplier_a_agreement.pdf`, p. 4; `causora_day1_mock.json` evidence EV-024).
5. **EV-027 — termination fee:** exact quote supports `$25,000` (`supplier_a_agreement.pdf`, p. 4; `causora_day1_mock.json` evidence EV-027). The API wire value is integer USD `25000` (`contract.terminationFeeUsd`).
6. **EV-020 — auto-renew:** the renewal clause is **conditional**, not unconditional: “If written notice is not received at least 60 days before renewal, the agreement automatically renews” (`supplier_a_agreement.pdf`, p. 4; `review_request.json` evidence EV-020). It must not be described as an unconditional lock.

The frozen mock’s `evidence` array contains EV-014, EV-019, EV-021, EV-024, and EV-027, but does not contain a separate EV-020 `EvidenceRecord` (`causora_day1_mock.json`, `evidence` array). EV-020 is nevertheless directly supported by the preserved PDF quote and the review request. This is a structured-record traceability gap for the linked auto-renew interpretation, not a contradiction in EV-024’s exact 60% evidence.

The frozen dates and notice record produce the demo assumption that the conditional clause is treated as locked: `decisionDate=2026-10-04`, `renewalDate=2026-11-18`, `noticeDeadline=2026-09-19`, `daysToRenewal=45`, and synthetic `noticeSent=false` (`causora_day1_mock.json` contract; `API_CONTRACT.md` §2, line 31). The notice CSV contains no valid written non-renewal notice entry through the deadline, but every row is marked `synthetic_mock_not_a_real_supplier_record` (`supplier_correspondence_log.csv`, rows dated 2026-09-01, 2026-09-19, and 2026-10-04). Thus this supports only the synthetic record assumption. It does **not** establish that no notice existed in the real world.

## Scope, provenance, and limitations

- `decisionDate` and `renewalDate` are accepted as explicit frozen demo assumptions, not values inferred from the PDF (`review_request.json` assumptions; `causora_day1_mock.json` contract).
- `noticeSent=false` is accepted only from the supplied synthetic notice register. It is not a real-world notice search or proof of absence.
- The PDF expressly says it is unsigned, unenforceable, fictional, and not associated with a real supplier (`supplier_a_agreement.pdf`, pp. 1–2 and footer/page labels); p. 5 calls the operational interpretation an interpretive synthetic assumption requiring counsel and source verification.
- No real contract verification, counsel review, supplier confirmation, or human signature was performed. `manually_verified=true` is not claimed.
- The API contract is a draft interface proposal and states that the static Day 1 website is a local mock; no backend implementation or production verification is claimed (`API_CONTRACT.md`, lines 1–5 and 80–89).
- The review does not validate historical demand, delivery history, inventory, simulation outputs, or business feasibility beyond the cited frozen values. In particular, locked forecast `26,000` is not historical-demand summation and is not a model output.
- Any production or real-contract use remains **pending** and requires human review of authentic signed contract terms, notice records, dates, and the applicable specification.
