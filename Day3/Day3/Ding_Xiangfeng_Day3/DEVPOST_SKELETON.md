## Project name

Causora

## Tagline

Evidence-to-decision intelligence for business choices that must be defended.

## One-sentence pitch

Causora turns contracts and operating data into explicit variables, compares fixed business options through a reproducible decision twin, and gives people an evidence-linked brief they can challenge before acting.

## Suggested story structure

1. Business decisions mix spreadsheets, contract clauses, and intuition.
2. Causora makes evidence inspectable and converts it into explicit constraints.
3. Code simulates predefined options under external scenarios.
4. CFO, COO, Risk, and Critic views make trade-offs challengeable.
5. The final Brief binds metrics to computation and keeps approval human.
