## Limitations and required follow-up

2. No human signature, reviewer identity, or manual verification is asserted. `manually_verified=true` must not be claimed. The review request has blank reviewer metadata.
3. The notice conclusion is limited to the supplied synthetic CSV. A real notice register, email system, or supplier record must be ingested and verified before any real-world conclusion.
4. The auto-renew result is conditional on the notice condition and the configured dates. It must not be represented as an unconditional locked renewal.
5. The six quote matches are not legal validation. Counsel/contract-owner review, source authorization, page-location verification where required, and production evidence review remain pending.
6. `decisionDate`, `renewalDate`, `noticeSent`, forecast basis, and 26,000 locked units are demo inputs/assumptions as identified above, not independently derived facts.
7. The API contract states `monteCarloRuns=0` denotes static Day 1 mock cells. No production simulation or live backend verification is established by these materials.
