## Limitations and required follow-up

1. This is a synthetic evidence review only. The PDF is unsigned, fictional, non-enforceable, and explicitly not for procurement.
2. `quoteMatched=true` and a `1.0` match score establish an exact preserved-quote match, not the truth or enforceability of a business fact; this distinction is explicit in the API contract.
3. `noticeSent=false` is supported only by the supplied synthetic notice register. It does not establish that no notice was sent in the real world.
4. The automatic-renewal conclusion is conditional on the notice condition and must not be described as an unconditional lock. A real notice register and contract-process verification remain pending.
5. Decision and renewal dates are accepted as explicit configured demo assumptions; they are not derived from the PDF.
6. The locked 26,000 forecast is a synthetic assumption, not historical-demand aggregation or a forecast-model output.
7. Human review of the actual authorized agreement, signatures, counterparties, legal interpretation, notice validity, and all production specification requirements remains pending. No manual verification or human signature is claimed.

## Conclusion

EV-021 is **acceptable as a clearly marked synthetic demonstration datum** with value `14%` (API wire value `0.14`). The evidence is exact and internally consistent, while all real-contract and human-review limitations remain explicitly pending.
