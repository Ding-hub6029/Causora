# EV-020 Review — Conditional Auto-Renew (Synthetic Demo)

## Scope and decision

- **Project:** EV-020 only
- **Field:** `auto_renew`
- **Expected value:** `conditional auto-renew clause present`
- **Synthetic decision:** `reviewed_synthetic`
- **Review basis:** The supplied agreement, frozen mock, notice CSV, review request, and API contract are internally consistent for the explicitly labelled synthetic demonstration. This is not approval of a real contract or a production decision.

The evidence supports a **conditional** auto-renewal clause, not an unconditional lock. The agreement says: “If written notice is not received at least 60 days before renewal, the agreement automatically renews.” It separately states a 24-month renewal at a 14% higher unit price. The condition is therefore the absence of timely written non-renewal notice; an earlier valid notice would change the outcome.

## Synthetic state and interpretation

For the frozen demonstration, `decisionDate="2026-10-04"` and `renewalDate="2026-11-18"` are explicit structured demo assumptions, not dates inferred from the PDF. The mock derives 45 days to renewal and compares it with the 60-day notice period. `noticeSent=false` is supported only by the supplied synthetic correspondence register; it does **not** establish that no notice was sent in the real world. Accordingly, the mock may label the synthetic scenario as locked (`contract.renewalLocked=true`), but this must not be generalized to a real contract.

The `lockedForecastUnits24m=26000` value is an explicit synthetic renewal-cycle forecast assumption. It is not a sum of historical demand and is not claimed to be a model-produced forecast. The API contract expressly distinguishes it from historical demand and states that the demo has `monteCarloRuns=0`.
