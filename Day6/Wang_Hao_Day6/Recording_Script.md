Preserve LIVE/CACHED labels. Waiting may be shortened with an honest duration caption, but never manufacture a live call.

| 150-160s | Hero / Brief | Every number is computed, every claim is traceable, every recommendation can be challenged. |

Fallback narration: This is a validated historical real run, replayed read-only. It is not a new AI call. Preserve the failure message and Matrix, then retry Boardroom only.

Closing: Causora is an inspectable decision workflow using synthetic demonstration data. Numerical results come from code. AI challenges the decision, and a human keeps the final call.
