# Screenshot and Recording Checklist

1. Hero / Data Intake: value proposition and inputs.
2. Matrix: explicit LIVE/CACHED label, nine cells and current scenario.
3. Formula Trace: D1 components, formula references and matching simulationId.
4. Evidence PDF: source page, highlight, EV identifier and version.
5. Critic: actual cross-functional findings, complete status and evidence links.
6. Architecture: computation, three bounded roles, Critic, validation, Brief and human choice.

Also retain the real Brief with enabled controls. Screenshots are in evidence. Do not relabel CACHED as LIVE. Verify source text and numbers. Never capture platform credentials.
