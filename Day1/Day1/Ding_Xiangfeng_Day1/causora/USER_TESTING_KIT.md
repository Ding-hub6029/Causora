## Day 2 moderator script

### Opening — 60 seconds

“Thank you for helping. This is an early mock prototype, not a finished system. Please think aloud. I am testing the product, not you. We will not use your personal data.”

### Tasks

1. **Find the source:** Locate the renewal notice period and tell me where the product says it came from.
2. **Inspect the assumption:** Switch to the *Demand −15%* scenario. What changed, and what did not change?
3. **Compare choices:** In the Matrix, identify which option appears to fit the default mock rules and explain why.
4. **Challenge the recommendation:** Open the Formula Trace and one Evidence record. Do you understand why the brief recommends D1?
5. **Keep control:** On the Brief, explain the difference between Approve, Reject, and Change assumptions.

### Closing questions

- What did you trust most? Why?
- What was confusing, slow, or unnecessary?
- At what moment did you feel you had enough evidence to decide?
- What one change would most improve this flow?
